# Exact hierarchy validation

Validation builds unit, lesson, and activity ownership maps. Every source location checks the complete tuple: unit exists; lesson exists and belongs to that unit; activity exists and belongs to that lesson and unit. The same ownership rule is applied to lecture segments, academic readings/assignments/assessments, package-level readings/assignments/assessments, lab sources, and formal activity references. Adversarial tests cover unknown and cross-wired references across those authored surfaces.
