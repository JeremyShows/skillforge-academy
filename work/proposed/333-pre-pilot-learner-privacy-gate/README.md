# WI-333 — Pre-Pilot Learner Privacy Safeguards

> **Status:** Proposed
> **Owner:** Governance steward; Privacy/Security and QA specialists review.
> **Depends on:** WI227 privacy/security baseline and WI332 reconciliation closeout.

## Intent

Make the privacy conditions for real learner participation an explicit prerequisite to WI228. This item covers the limited, local-first controlled-pilot boundary. It does not claim legal compliance and does not design accounts, hosted analytics, school administration, synchronization, or other future collection.

WI312 retains the broader institutional, hosted, and minor-data architecture. It is not a prerequisite for WI228. WI228 cannot be activated until WI333 is completed with evidence, as well as the separate candidate-readiness requirements it already depends on.

## Scope and boundaries

- Pilot participant notice, consent, voluntary feedback, minimization, and stop conditions.
- Pseudonymous support and feedback handling where practical.
- Redaction and exclusion of real learner information from public repositories and evidence.
- A pause-and-review boundary for minors or institution-controlled data collection.
- A privacy review of the WI310 runbook before recruitment.

No telemetry, account, cloud, or hosted-data capability is added by this work item. Regulatory applicability depends on actual users, deployment, collection, and controls; repository wording alone cannot establish compliance.

## Closeout

Keep the item proposed until WI332 closes. Before WI228 begins, satisfy every criterion with evidence and confirm the exact candidate and privacy posture in the pilot runbook.
